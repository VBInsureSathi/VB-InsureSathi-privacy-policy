# Privacy Policy for VB InsureSathi

**Last Updated:** 22 November 2026  
**App Name:** VB InsureSathi

## 1. About Us

VB InsureSathi is an Android application for motor insurance and other insurance services. Customers can enquire, chat, share documents, receive quotes, and purchase policies through in-app chat and WhatsApp communication.

## 2. Information We Collect

- **Personal Information:** Name, mobile number, and email address, if provided
- **Policy Details:** Vehicle number, RC details, previous policy details, date of birth, and address
- **Documents:** RC book, Aadhaar card, PAN card, driving licence, previous insurance policy, vehicle photographs, and KYC documents
- **Chat Data:** Messages sent through the app chat and WhatsApp chat for enquiries and support
- **Device Information:** Device model, Android version, and app usage data used to improve performance
- **Login Data:** Name and mobile number

We do not collect OTPs, UPI PINs, banking passwords, or card CVV numbers.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Generate insurance quotes through insurer portals
- Issue motor, health, life, travel, home, and personal accident policies
- Communicate with you through app chat and WhatsApp
- Collect documents required for policy issuance
- Provide renewal reminders and customer support
- Improve app services and resolve technical issues

## 4. Documents Required for Policy Issuance

For quote generation and policy issuance, you may be asked to provide:

- RC or Vehicle Registration Certificate
- Previous policy copy for renewal or NCB purposes
- Aadhaar card or PAN card for KYC
- Driving licence
- Address proof
- Vehicle photographs, if required by the insurer

Documents are shared only for insurance-related purposes.

## 5. Sharing With Third Parties

Your information and documents are shared only with:

- Insurance companies and insurer portals for quote generation and policy issuance
- IRDAI-licensed insurers and their authorised systems
- WhatsApp (Meta), when you choose to communicate or share documents through WhatsApp

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 6. WhatsApp Communication

When you use the WhatsApp option in the app, you will be redirected to WhatsApp to chat with 8008542676. WhatsApp messages are governed by WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy. Please do not share banking passwords or other sensitive financial credentials through WhatsApp.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely and accessed only by the VB InsureSathi team for authorised policy-related work.
- We use reasonable security measures to protect your documents.
- Your mobile number is visible only to the application owner or authorised team members for service purposes.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your policy details and documents as required for policy service, renewals, and applicable insurance regulations. You may request deletion of your chat data by contacting us.

## 9. Your Rights

You may:

- Request information about the data we hold about you
- Request correction of your details
- Request deletion of your account data
- Stop receiving WhatsApp communications at any time

To make a request, contact us on 8008542676.

## 10. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under 18 years of age. Insurance policies are issued only to adults.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be shown in the app.
